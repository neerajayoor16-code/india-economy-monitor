What it is

The benchmark interest rate at which the Reserve Bank of India lends short-term money to commercial banks.

Why it matters

Central banks raise the repo rate to cool down fast-rising prices and slow excessive borrowing. Starting a rate-hike cycle is a classic late-stage boom signal.

What it means for a household

The repo rate sets the base for loan rates. When RBI raises it, EMIs on new and floating-rate home and car loans usually go up within a few months; when it cuts, they go down.
