Railway Freight Loading: +8.32% YoY in Oct 2026.

Good right now: freight growth of 3% or more signals active trade.

What it is

The weight of goods that Indian Railways carries in a month, such as coal, iron ore, cement, food grains and containers, compared with the same month one year ago.

Why it matters

Factories, power plants and traders move most bulk goods by rail. When more goods move, more is being produced and sold right now. Freight data comes out within days of the month ending, so it shows current activity sooner than most official statistics.

What it means for a household

When a steel plant in Odisha gets more orders, it books more rakes of iron ore and coal. That extra loading shows up in the railway freight number weeks before it shows up in factory output data.
