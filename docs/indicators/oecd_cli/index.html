What it is

An international forward-looking indicator built to spot economic turning points 6 to 9 months ahead. A reading of 100 represents India's long-term normal trend.

Why it matters

When the index sits above 100 and keeps ticking higher, it signals the economy is expanding above its normal pace with no broad downturn in sight yet.

What it means for a household

The OECD's leading index combines several early signals into one number. Above 100 means activity is above its long-term trend; the direction shows where it is heading.
