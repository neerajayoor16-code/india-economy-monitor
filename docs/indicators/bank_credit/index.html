Bank Credit Growth: 19.54% YoY on Oct 31, 2026.

Watch closely right now: healthy credit growth band 12% to 16%.

What it is

Measures how fast Indian banks are giving out new loans to families (home, car, personal loans) and businesses compared to a year ago.

Why it matters

Healthy loan growth (around 12% to 16%) fuels business expansion. However, when lending grows much faster than bank deposits for months, banks stretch their funding and credit booms can eventually trigger overheating.

What it means for a household

Fast credit growth means banks are lending freely for homes, cars and businesses. It helps growth, but very fast lending can push up prices and risky loans.
