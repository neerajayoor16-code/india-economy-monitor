Unemployment Rate (PLFS): 4.9% in Oct 2026, down from 5.0% in Sep 2026.

Good right now: low and not rising.

What it is

Shows the percentage of people actively looking for work who do not currently have a job.

Why it matters

Hiring and training workers is expensive, so companies only expand headcount aggressively once they are confident business growth is real. Falling unemployment confirms a healthy expansion has been underway.

What it means for a household

A falling unemployment rate means more people looking for work find it, so fresh graduates get offers sooner and workers have more power to ask for a raise.
