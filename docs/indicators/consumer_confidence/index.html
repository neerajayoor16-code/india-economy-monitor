What it is

The Reserve Bank of India's Future Expectations Index: how households in 19 cities expect the economy, jobs, prices, their income and their spending to change over the next year. Above 100 means more optimists than pessimists.

Why it matters

Household spending is more than half of India's economy. When families expect better incomes and jobs, they buy more homes, vehicles and appliances in the months that follow. The index is computed as 100 plus the average of five net responses, the formula RBI publishes with the survey.

What it means for a household

A family in Pune that expects a salary rise next year is more likely to book a new two-wheeler or start home renovation now. Many such decisions together move this index.
