Good right now: index above 100 means more optimists than pessimists; 110 or more is clearly positive.

What it is

The Reserve Bank of India's Business Expectations Index: how manufacturing companies expect production, orders, jobs and profits to change in the next quarter. Above 100 means more companies expect improvement than decline.

Why it matters

Companies plan production, hiring and investment on what they expect, not on last month's results. Their expectations often turn before actual output does, which makes this a leading signal.

What it means for a household

When a textile mill in Tiruppur expects more export orders next quarter, it hires workers and buys yarn now. RBI's survey asks companies like this about their plans.
