About this site

India Economic Health Monitor explains where India's economy stands in the business cycle, in plain English. It tracks 16 official indicators in three steps: what already happened (lagging), what is happening now (coincident), and what may come next (leading).

Every number is taken from an official source and linked on its page. Numbers update automatically after each official release and pass automatic checks first. See the methodology for the exact rules.
