Methodology

This page explains where every number comes from, how it is checked, and how the signals are decided. The rules below are the same rules the code uses.

How numbers are collected

A program checks every official source every 30 minutes on weekdays from 09:30 to 18:30 IST, and runs a full check once a day. Sources: MoSPI (CPI, WPI, factory output, unemployment, GDP), the Reserve Bank of India (repo rate, bond yields, Nifty 50 as republished by RBI, bank credit, consumer and business surveys), OECD (leading indicator), PIB (core industries, railway freight), GSTN (GST revenue) and SIAM (vehicle sales).

How numbers are checked

A new number goes live only if it is a real number inside a sane range for that indicator, its period is not older than the number already shown, and it did not jump more than a set limit since the last value. If a check fails, the old number stays and the owner gets an alert. Where a source states its own growth rate, the program computes the rate itself and the two must match.

How the phase is decided

The phase follows the OECD's rule on its composite leading indicator (CLI) for India: above 100 and rising means expansion; above 100 and falling means slowdown; below 100 and falling means contraction; below 100 and rising means recovery. During an expansion, if retail inflation, wholesale inflation or bank credit shows Watch closely or a Warning sign, the site calls it a late-stage expansion.

How each signal is decided

CPI Retail Inflation

Good 2% to 4.5%. Watch closely 4.5% to 6%, or below 2%. Warning sign above 6% (RBI target 4%, tolerance band 2% to 6%).

WPI Wholesale Inflation

Good -1% to 4%. Watch closely 4% to 8%, or below -1%. Warning sign above 8%.

Unemployment Rate (PLFS)

Good when 5.5% or lower and not rising by 0.3 points or more. Watch closely when above 5.5% or rising by 0.3 points or more. Warning sign above 7% or rising by 0.8 points or more.

Bank Credit Growth

Good 12% to 16%. Watch closely 8% to 12% or 16% to 20%. Warning sign below 8% or above 20%.

RBI Repo Rate (Policy Rate)

Judged on the real policy rate (repo rate minus CPI inflation). Good 0.5 to 2.5 points. Watch closely -1 to 0.5 or 2.5 to 4 points. Warning sign outside -1 to 4 points.

Index of Core Industries (ICI)

Good at 4% growth or more. Watch closely 0% to 4%. Warning sign below 0%.

Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

Good at 4% growth or more. Watch closely 0% to 4%. Warning sign below 0%.

Railway Freight Loading

Good at 3% growth or more. Watch closely 0% to 3%. Warning sign below 0%.

GST Collections

Judged on gross GST growth. Good at 8% or more. Watch closely 0% to 8%. Warning sign below 0%.

Passenger Vehicle Sales

Judged on sales growth. Good at 5% or more. Watch closely 0% to 5%. Warning sign below 0%.

Real GDP Growth (Reference)

Good at 6.5% real growth or more. Watch closely 5% to 6.5%. Warning sign below 5%.

Nifty 50 (Stock Market)

Judged on the change over about 3 months. Good when down less than 5% (or up). Watch closely when down 5% to 10%. Warning sign when down more than 10%. Needs 3 months of history; until then it shows Watch closely.

Bond Yield Spread (10Y vs Repo)

10-year government bond yield minus the repo rate. Good at 100 basis points or more. Watch closely 0 to 100. Warning sign below 0 (an inverted curve).

Consumer Confidence (RBI)

Good at 110 or more. Watch closely 100 to 110. Warning sign below 100 (more pessimists than optimists).

Business Expectations (RBI)

Good at 110 or more. Watch closely 100 to 110. Warning sign below 100.

OECD Leading Index (CLI)

Good when above 100 and rising. Watch closely when above 100 but falling, or below 100 but rising. Warning sign when below 100 and falling.

How the text is written

The short texts on each card are written from the numbers. When AI writing is on, an AI model drafts the text, and it goes live only if every number in it is in the official data, it gives no investment advice and no certain predictions, it describes the direction of change correctly, and a second independent check confirms it matches the data. Otherwise the card uses plain fill-in sentences.

Limits

Signals are simple rules, not forecasts. Official numbers are often revised; the site shows the latest published value. Nothing on this site is investment advice.
