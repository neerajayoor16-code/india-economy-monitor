Keep it fresh

When to refresh & official data portals

Most Indian economic reports arrive in two monthly waves rather than on the 1st of the month.

6th to 8th of every month

Primary monthly refresh

Best main check-in. Captures GST collections (1st), HSBC PMIs (1st to 3rd), auto sales (1st to 3rd), Core Industries, and OECD CLI (around the 8th).

16th of every month

Mid-month check

Captures CPI retail inflation (12th), IIP factory output (12th), WPI wholesale inflation (14th), PLFS jobs data (around the 15th), and RBI credit data.

1st week of Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec

Quarterly anchor

Captures MoSPI's quarterly Real GDP report (released on the last working day of Feb, May, Aug, Nov) alongside bi-monthly RBI rate decisions.

How this page updates
Every 30 minutes on weekdays (10:00 to 18:30 IST), a program reads each official source above. A new number goes live only after automatic checks: it must be a real number in a sane range, not older than the number shown, and not a sudden jump. Every card shows its source and release period. If a source fails, the card keeps its last number and shows a warning when it gets too old. The signals use fixed rules, explained on the methodology page.

30-Second Guide

How to read this dashboard

Think of India's economy like driving a car on a highway. Economists look at three types of clues together to know where we are:

1
Past / Lagging Indicators = The Rearview Mirror
Inflation, unemployment, and central bank rates change after a trend is already underway. They confirm what the economy has been doing.
2
Present / Coincident Indicators = The Speedometer
Factory output, GST collections, car sales, and GDP show how fast the economy is moving right now.
3
Future / Leading Indicators = The Headlights
Stock markets, bond yield spreads, business surveys (PMIs), and composite leading indexes point to where the road is turning next.
